Smoke test 10b: echo and barge-in (manual)

Your microphone listens while the "agent" talks through your speakers. The agent's own voice must not trigger an interruption; your voice must. Use speakers, not headphones, for the hardest case. Nothing leaves this machine.

  1. Start mic and allow microphone access.
  2. Round 1 (stay silent): click Play agent voice and say nothing until it stops. False barge-ins should stay at 0.
  3. Round 2 (interrupt): click Play agent voice again and start talking halfway through. The agent should get quieter right away, then stop completely once you've spoken for ~¼ s. A cough or click should only dip the volume briefly.
